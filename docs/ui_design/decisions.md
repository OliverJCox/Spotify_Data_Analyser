# Interface decisions

## Colour scheme

Similar to that of Spotify. Dark grey backgrounds, slightly lighter pannels and green accents.

## Charts/Graphs

When no artists are searched for the charts that will be shown are:
- Top 10 artists 
- Top 10 tracks

**These may not actually show 10 depending on filters**

When artist(s) has been selected charts that will be shown are:
- Listening over time
- Pie chart of share of total listening time
